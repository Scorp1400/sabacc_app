HOW TO PLAY

  • Goal: be the last player with chips. Winner takes the whole pot (4x the stake).
  • Cards: 2 each — Sand (yellow, 1–6) and Blood (red, 1–6), plus wild Sylops and Impostors.
  • Each round: 3 turns each. Draw costs 1 chip (take from the deck or the discard pile, then discard one). Stand is free.
  • Showdown: lowest pair wins (pair of 1s best). No pair — smallest gap wins. Sylop copies your other card. Two Sylops is Pure Sabacc and beats everything. Impostor takes a die value — you pick from 2 dice (3 with Loaded Die).
  • Chips: losers pay their card gap (a losing pair pays 0). Winners get back the chips they spent drawing. At 0 chips you're eliminated.
  • Shift tokens: draft 3 before the game, one use each. Play one at the start of your turn, then Draw or Stand. Bots get 3 random ones.
  • Money: everyone antes the stake. Win the game, take all of it. Your bank follows you between games.
