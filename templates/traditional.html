HOW TO PLAY

  • Goal: finish closest to +23 or −23. Exactly ±23 is a Sabacc. Over is bombed out. Bust all three bots to clear the table.
  • 3 rounds. Each round: shift dice first (roll doubles and it's a SHIFT! — one random card in every hand twists by up to ±3), then one action each, then a betting round.
  • Your action: Draw adds a card (max 5 in hand). Swap replaces one (tap Swap, then tap the card). Stand keeps your hand. Fold drops out. No mid-hand busts — you can always draw back with a negative-suit card.
  • Suits: Coins and Sabers count positive (gold). Flasks and Staves count negative (red).
  • Betting: Check, Bet 20, Call, one Raise to 40, or Fold. No side pots — bets are capped at your stack.
  • Showdown: Idiot's Array (Idiot + any 2 + any 3) beats everything, then Pure Sabacc (two Sylops), then natural ±23, then closest. Sylops are declared 0 / +15 / −15 at showdown — you pick, bots pick best. All ties split.
  • Two pots: the main pot pays every hand. The Sabacc pot (seeded at 100) pays only on Idiot's Array, Pure Sabacc, or natural ±23 — and if everyone bombs out, the main pot feeds it instead.
  • Ante is 10. Your bank follows you; each table you buy in for up to 300. Bot bankrolls carry between hands — a bot under 10 credits is busted and sits out. Go broke at the table and you buy back in from your bank.
